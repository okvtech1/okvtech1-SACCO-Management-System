Privacy Policy

Effective date: · Applies to OKV SACCO Subscribe and OKV Technology Consults' related sites and apps

OKV Technology Consults ("OKV", "we", "us") built OKV SACCO Subscribe to help savings & credit cooperatives manage their members, contributions, loans, repayments and dividends. This Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how it's stored, and the rights you and your organization's members have over it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information at three levels:

  • Organization & Admin data: organization/business name, admin full name, email, phone number, and a hashed password, collected when you sign up.
  • Staff (User) data: username, email, roles, and a hashed password, entered by your organization's Admin when creating staff logins.
  • Member data: names, phone numbers, emails, and financial records (savings, shares, loans, repayments, dividends) that your organization's staff enter into the system on your members' behalf.
  • Billing data: the name, email, phone, plan chosen, and payment-proof screenshot submitted when upgrading or renewing a subscription. We do not collect or store full card or bank account credentials ourselves — where a payment gateway (e.g. Paystack, Flutterwave) is enabled, card handling happens directly with that gateway, not on our servers.
  • Usage & device data: basic technical information (browser type, device type, approximate sync timestamps) needed to keep the offline-sync engine working correctly.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and operate your organization's account and staff logins.
  • To let your team record, sync, and report on member, contribution, loan, repayment and dividend records.
  • To process subscription payments, send renewal/trial reminders, and confirm upgrades.
  • To send the messages and statements your Admin or authorized staff choose to send to members (Email/SMS/WhatsApp).
  • To provide support, respond to enquiries, and send service-related notices (e.g. payment confirmations, password resets).
  • To maintain security, detect abuse, and keep the service reliable.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your organization's or members' data to advertise to them.

3. Where & How Data Is Stored

OKV SACCO Subscribe is built on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script. Each organization's business records (members, contributions, loans, repayments, dividends) are stored in a dedicated spreadsheet created for that organization alone — no organization can access another's spreadsheet. Account credentials, subscription details, and platform-wide settings are stored separately in our master control spreadsheet. On your device, the app also keeps a local offline copy (via your browser's IndexedDB) so you can keep working without an internet connection; this local copy syncs to your organization's spreadsheet once you're back online.

4. Sharing With Third Parties

We share data only where necessary to run the service:

  • Google (Sheets/Apps Script/Gmail): our hosting and data-storage infrastructure.
  • Payment processors (e.g. Paystack, Flutterwave, Remita) — only if and when your organization chooses to pay via one of these, and only with the data needed to process that transaction.
  • SMS/WhatsApp providers (e.g. Termii) — only when your organization sends a message to members via SMS or WhatsApp, and only with the recipient's phone number and message content needed to deliver it.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities — only where required by law.

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise trade personal data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain your organization's data for as long as your account remains active, plus a reasonable period afterward to allow for reactivation, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. You may request deletion of your organization's account and data at any time by contacting us (see Section 13); some records may be retained longer where required by law (e.g. financial/tax record-keeping obligations).

6. Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before storage. Access to each organization's spreadsheet is scoped so that staff only see the records their role and Admin settings permit. We restrict administrative access to the platform's infrastructure to authorized personnel only. No system is 100% secure, and we encourage strong, unique passwords and prompt reporting of any suspected unauthorized access.

7. Your Rights

Under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and applicable data protection regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you or your organization.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent to non-essential communications at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13.

8. Member Data — Your Organization's Responsibilities

Your organization (the Admin and staff you authorize) acts as the data controller for the member records you enter into the system — you are responsible for having a lawful basis to collect and process your members' data, and for informing your members how their information is used. OKV acts as a data processor for that member data: we process it only to provide the service to you, and only in line with your instructions given through the system (e.g. sending a statement you chose to send).

9. Cookies & Local Storage

OKV SACCO Subscribe does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. The app uses your browser's local storage and IndexedDB purely to keep you signed in and to store your organization's records for offline use — this data stays on your device and syncs only to your organization's own spreadsheet.

10. Children's Privacy

OKV SACCO Subscribe is intended for use by cooperative organizations, their staff, and their adult members. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

11. International Data Transfer

Because our infrastructure runs on Google's global cloud services, data may be processed on servers outside Nigeria. Where this happens, it is subject to Google's own security and data protection commitments, and we take reasonable steps to ensure your data remains protected to a standard consistent with this Policy.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the service or applicable law. We'll update the "Effective date" above when we do, and material changes will be communicated to Admins by email or an in-app notice.
