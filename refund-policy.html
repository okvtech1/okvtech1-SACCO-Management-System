30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to be confident OKV SACCO Subscribe fits how your SACCO operates before you commit long-term — that's what our free trial and this guarantee are for.

Free Trial — Nothing to Refund

Every organization starts with a 7-day free trial, and no card is required to start it. Since nothing is charged during the trial, there is nothing to refund if you decide not to continue — your account simply stays inactive after the trial ends unless you choose a plan.

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you're not satisfied after subscribing to your first paid plan, contact us within 30 days of that payment being confirmed and we'll refund it in full — no long explanations needed. This guarantee applies once per organization, to the first paid subscription only.

How to Request a Refund

  • Contact us using the details below within 30 days of your first paid subscription being confirmed.
  • Quote your Organization ID (shown on your dashboard's Subscription tab) and the email or phone number used at signup.
  • We'll confirm your request and process the refund back to the same payment method or account used, typically within 5–7 business days.

Renewals & Subsequent Payments

The 30-day guarantee applies to your organization's first paid subscription. Renewal payments (for continuing an existing subscription into a new billing cycle) are not covered by this guarantee, since you've already had the opportunity to evaluate the service — but we're always happy to discuss a fair resolution if something went wrong on our end (e.g. a billing error or a service outage that materially affected your use).

Downgrades & Plan Changes

If you switch from a higher to a lower plan, or change billing cycle, the change applies from your next renewal — we don't provide partial/prorated refunds for the unused portion of a current billing cycle outside the 30-day guarantee window above.

Non-Refundable Circumstances

  • Accounts suspended or terminated for violating our Terms of Service.
  • Refund requests made more than 30 days after the first paid subscription was confirmed.
  • Any subscription beyond the first paid one for a given organization (see "Renewals" above).
