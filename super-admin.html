User Manual

Guides for running the platform

Organizations

The Organizations tab lists every subscribed cooperative, their plan, and subscription status. Use the Suspend/Activate toggle to control access without deleting an organization's data, and open an org to see its Admin's contact details.

Capacity

Each organization's data lives in its own Google Sheet, capped at 10,000,000 cells. This tab shows usage, growth rate, and estimated time to capacity for every tenant — Healthy, Monitor, or Action Needed — so you can archive or split a tenant's data before it becomes a problem.

Payments

Review bank-transfer submissions here. Approving one updates that organization's subscription automatically; rejecting one notifies the Admin to resubmit.

Announcements

Message specific Admins or every organization on the platform at once — useful for maintenance notices or new-feature announcements.

Branding, Plans, Payment Methods & Integrations

These control what every organization sees platform-wide: your logo and system name, the pricing plans offered at signup, which bank/payment details appear on the Subscription tab, and which SMS/WhatsApp provider powers member messaging.
