Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need internet to use the app?
No. Every register works fully offline. Anything you add, edit or delete is saved on your device immediately and syncs automatically once you're back online.
Can my staff use it on their own phones?
Yes — each staff member gets their own login and role, and can install the app on any Android phone, iPhone, tablet, or computer.
What happens after my 7-day free trial ends?
You'll see a countdown and an Upgrade prompt right on your dashboard before it ends. Choose a plan on the Pricing page to keep going without losing any data.
Is my organization's data visible to other SACCOs on the platform?
No. Every organization's data is fully isolated — no organization can ever see another's records.
What's the difference between the Basic and Professional plans?
Basic suits small, single-branch cooperatives with a couple of staff logins. Professional removes the member/staff limits and adds priority support and data export — see the Pricing page for full details.
What if I'm not satisfied after subscribing?
You're covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee on your first paid subscription — reach out and we'll refund you, no long explanations needed.
Can I message my members directly from the system?
Yes — Admins and assigned staff (Managers, Membership and Finance Officers) can send announcements or personalized statements to one member or all of them at once, by Email, SMS or WhatsApp.
How do I upgrade or renew my subscription?
From your dashboard's Subscription tab, choose your plan and pay by bank transfer, then upload your payment screenshot. We're notified immediately, and you'll get a status email within the hour — once confirmed, your plan updates automatically with your new renewal date.
